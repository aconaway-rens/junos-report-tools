Routes have no zone of their own — the join is route → egress interface → zone, so a route is attributed to a zone only when its next-hop resolves to an interface that appears in the zone output. Discard, reject, and receive routes have no egress interface and are listed without a zone. Paste both captures from the same box at the same time; a stale route paste will attribute traffic to the wrong place.
